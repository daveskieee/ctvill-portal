1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This audit and remediation deliverable completes the end-to-end professional
minimalist ERP redesign and centralized Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) navigation
across all modules of the CTVILL Commercial Fit-Out ERP application.

All 22 functional modules, inner tables, forms, charts, modals, tabs, buttons,
and filters were audited and standardized to a cohesive, high-density, restrained
commercial ERP design system (dark slate palette, slate-800 borders, amber primary
action accents, mono-tagged typography, and zero decorative bloat).

The sidebar navigation was replaced with a centralized, verified single source of
truth (src/config/navigation.ts) strictly enforcing authorized module access for
all 5 primary system roles (ADMIN, ENGINEER / PROJECT_MANAGER, TIMEKEEPER, FINANCE,
and CLIENT).

Backend routes, database records, Phase 1 security controls, Phase 2 Multi-Provider
AI architecture (Gemini -> Groq -> Rule-Based), and Phase 3 database integration
remain 100% untouched and fully intact. Phase 4 has NOT been started.

Role-Specific Invariants & Separation of Duties (SoD):
1. TIMEKEEPER:
   - Strictly restricted to 2 modules: Timekeeper Attendance Log and Account Settings.
   - Completely excluded from payroll calculation, wages, project financials, Gantt,
     Kanban, site diary, and subcontractor payables.
2. FINANCE:
   - Authorized for 12 financial, audit, and ledger modules.
   - Separation of Duties: Exclusively performs weekly payroll finalization and cash
     advance (vale) management. Holds read-only audit oversight for subcontractor
     muster roll-calls and attendance logs.
   - Strictly excluded from field execution boards (Gantt timeline, Kanban, site diary,
     RFIs, workforce messenger, change orders).
3. ENGINEER / PROJECT_MANAGER:
   - Authorized for 14 operational and engineering modules.
   - Field execution authority for Gantt CPM, daily site diaries, RFIs, blueprints,
     and material permits.
   - Strictly excluded from wage masterlists, payroll runs, subcontractor disbursals,
     and progress billing releases.
4. CLIENT:
   - Strictly scoped in API (/api/all-data) and UI to their own assigned project.
   - Authorized for 4 modules: Project Profile Hub, Blueprint Documents, Submitted
     Quotes/Estimates, and Personal Account Settings.
   - Completely hidden: All internal staff rosters, timekeeping, contractor audits,
     financial ledgers, RFIs, and site diaries.
5. UNAUTHORIZED / Unknown:
   - Safely defaults to weight 0, resolving only to Account Settings. Prevents
     privilege escalation.
